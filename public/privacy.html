What we collect

  • Account information: the username and password you register with (your password is stored as a one-way cryptographic hash — we cannot see or recover your actual password).
  • Chat memory: if you tell Slippers your name, or chat about certain topics, that context (name, mood, current topic) is stored against your account so she can pick up conversations where you left off. The actual text of your messages is not sent to our server or stored in our database — the conversation itself happens entirely in your browser; only this small derived summary is saved.
  • Chatlog viewer: the "read the chatlog" feature in the chat interface is held only in your browser's memory for the current visit — it is never transmitted to us, and disappears when you refresh or close the page.
  • Technical data: your IP address, used only for rate-limiting (preventing abuse of registration/login) and for spam/bot verification (see Cookies & Third Parties below).
  • Session cookie: a single cookie that keeps you logged in between page loads. See the Cookies section below.

Why we collect it

We use this information solely to operate the chatbot service itself: authenticating you, remembering context within your conversations, and preventing abuse (spam registrations, brute-force login attempts). We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers, and we do not use your data for any purpose beyond running this service.

Cookies & third parties

We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

When you register or log in, your browser communicates with Cloudflare Turnstile, a bot-prevention service, to confirm you're a real person. This means your IP address is shared with Cloudflare during that specific check. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for how they handle that data.

Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information — you can do this yourself at any time by asking Slippers to "forget me" in chat, which erases your stored name, mood, and topic immediately, or by contacting us to request full account deletion
  • Object to certain processing, or lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your information has been mishandled

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details above.

How long we keep information

Account and chat memory data is kept for as long as your account is active. To avoid holding data longer than necessary, accounts that have had no activity for 120 days are automatically and permanently deleted. If you'd like your account deleted sooner, contact us using the details above.

Security

Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text), and access to the underlying database is restricted. As with any online service, we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable technical steps to protect your information.

Children's privacy

This service is not directed at children, and requires users to be 18 years or older, confirmed at registration, in line with our Terms & Conditions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
